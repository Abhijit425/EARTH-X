# EARTH-X — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

EARTH-X ("the app") is an Earth exploration app for Android. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

## Information we collect
**None.** The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking SDKs. The developer does not receive any information about you or your device.

## Data stored on your device
Favourite places, collections, recent searches, settings and cached map/weather/earthquake data are stored only on your device (app-private storage). Screenshots you take are saved to your device's Pictures/EARTH-X folder. Uninstalling the app (or "Clear storage") removes the app data.

## Network requests to public data services
To show live information the app downloads public data directly from:
- **Open-Meteo** (api.open-meteo.com) — weather. When you select a place, its latitude/longitude (the place you tapped on the globe, **not** your device's GPS location) is sent to retrieve that place's weather. A fixed global grid of coordinates is requested for the weather layers.
- **U.S. Geological Survey** (earthquake.usgs.gov) — earthquake feeds.
- **NASA GIBS** (gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov) — satellite imagery for the map area you are viewing.

These services receive standard technical information that any internet request includes (such as your IP address and the app's user-agent "EARTH-X"). Their own privacy policies apply. The app does not use your device location and does not request the location permission.

## Permissions
- **Internet / network state** — to download the public data above and to show the offline indicator.
- **Storage (Android 9 and older only)** — to save screenshots you take.

## Children
The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
